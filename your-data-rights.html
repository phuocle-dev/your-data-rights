Your Data Rights

Last updated: November 3, 2026

We respect your privacy and want you to understand the choices available to you. This page explains how to exercise privacy rights relating to information handled by VoiceNudge.

Information handled by VoiceNudge

VoiceNudge does not require an account. Reminder information and voice recordings you create are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to VoiceNudge servers.

The app may process limited technical and usage information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, an anonymous device identifier, and reminder notification events. Limited technical or notification-tracking information may be handled by third-party services to help us monitor app reliability and improve the app.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access: Ask what personal information we process about you.
  • Request deletion: Ask us to delete personal information associated with you, where deletion is applicable and technically available.
  • Request correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.
  • Ask a question or raise a concern: Contact us about how your information is handled.

How to submit a request

To exercise a privacy right, contact the developer using the contact information available on the VoiceNudge App Store listing. Please describe your request and include enough information for us to understand and respond to it. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

We will review your request and respond in accordance with applicable privacy laws. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request relates to you before taking action.

Deleting information stored on your device

Voice recordings and reminder data: These are stored locally on your device. You can delete reminders and recordings within the app where those controls are available. Uninstalling VoiceNudge generally removes the app's locally stored data, subject to your device's backup and operating-system behavior.

Third-party technical data

Some limited technical or notification-tracking information may be processed by third-party services used to support app analytics, reliability, and diagnostics. If you want to ask about or request deletion of information handled by these services, contact us using the method above. We will assess what information is available to us and what action can be taken.
